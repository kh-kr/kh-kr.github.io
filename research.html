Research

Five threads run through my work. Each one starts from the same question — what happens when a star in a cluster stops behaving like its neighbours?

Blue stragglers, blue lurkers & yellow stragglers

Stars that appear younger than their cluster should allow, rejuvenated by mass transfer, mergers, or collisions. I characterise these populations, their hot companions, and formation pathways using multiwavelength photometry from AstroSat/UVIT and Swift/UVOT to infrared data, spectroscopy, and light-curve modelling.

Dynamical ages of open clusters

Blue stragglers sink toward cluster centres as clusters relax, making them natural dynamical clocks. In a series of papers, I used various tools to measure the dynamical ages of open clusters.

A⁺ parameter, Paper I Paper II Four old open clusters Blue stragglers in seven clusters

Rotation & extended main-sequence turnoffs

Why do coeval stars fan out at the turnoff of young and intermediate-age clusters (younger than 2 Gyr)? I investigate how rotation and braking of intermediate-mass stars (1.5–8 solar masses) sculpt extended main-sequence turnoffs across Galactic open clusters using N-body simulations, multiwavelength photometry, Chandra X-ray data, and spectroscopy.

Extended turnoffs cencus in 53 Galactic open clusters A merger origin for the blue main sequence of NGC 3532 Early angular momentum loss in intermediate-mass stars

Variable stars & eclipsing binaries

Time-series photometry from TESS, Kepler, and Gaia revealed eclipsing binaries and pulsators in clusters. I use light-curve modelling and radial-velocity monitoring of eclipsing blue stragglers to constrain their masses, luminosities, and evolutionary state.

Variables and X-ray sources in three open clusters

Ongoing, with spectroscopy awarded on VLT/GIRAFFE, Gemini/GHOST and Gemini North/MAROON-X.

Machine-learning cluster membership

Reliable membership is the foundation of every cluster study. ML-MOC, a kNN + Gaussian-mixture approach to determining open cluster members from Gaia data without hand-tuned cuts, is now applied across dozens of open cluster studies. ML-MOC based members also led discovery/verification of tidal tails and halo in several open cluster.
